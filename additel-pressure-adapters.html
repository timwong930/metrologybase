Pressure and connection safety: “HP” and “Autoclave” mean the 1/4 HP high-pressure cone-and-thread connection (Autoclave M-250-C) in this catalog; they do not mean all HP-ended adapters are rated to 60,000 psi. The adapter, mating port, seals, hoses, pump, and entire assembly must each be suitable for the service. The lowest-rated component limits the system. One listed hose-barb fitting is rated only 150 psi. “BSP” is Additel’s catalog wording; confirm BSPP vs. BSPT and the sealing geometry with Additel rather than assuming interchangeability.

Which kit should I start with?

  • ADT102 (25 pieces): Common threaded NPT, BSP, and metric conversions with a 1/4 NPT male source end; includes a hose, 90-degree quick connector, and low-pressure hose barb. Other source threads may be available by request; confirm SKU and pressure rating with Additel. Additel's current product page excludes pumps 949, 959, and 960; verify pump model.
  • ADT103-NPT, -BSP, -M20 (10 pieces each): Hand-tight female quick connectors with 1/4 NPT, 1/4 BSP, or M20×1.5 male pump ends, respectively. Additel excludes the 949 pump.
  • ADT104-HP (17 pieces; 15 unique part numbers): 1/4 HP male (Autoclave M-250-C) source for Additel 949/959/960 pumps. Each adapter has its own 15,000, 40,000, or 60,000 psi maximum.

Important: A 40,000 psi adapter is not automatically suitable at 40,000 psi when used with a lower-rated hose, pump, gauge, or pressure port. Check seal type, media, connection preparation, manufacturer instructions, and operating conditions before pressurizing.
