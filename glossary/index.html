Metrology glossary

Understand the term before the conversation moves on.

Short, plain-language definitions for the terminology used in calibration, specifications, procedures, and technical discussions.

Accuracy

How close a measurement result is to an accepted reference value.

Calibration

A comparison that establishes the relationship between a measurement result and a reference.

DUT

Device under test — the instrument or system being evaluated.

Precision

How closely repeated measurements agree with one another.

Resolution

The smallest change in indication or output that can be meaningfully distinguished.

Traceability

A documented chain connecting a measurement result to recognized references.

Tolerance

The permitted deviation from a specified target, nominal value, or limit.

TUR

Test uncertainty ratio — a comparison between DUT tolerance and calibration-process uncertainty.

Uncertainty

A quantified expression of doubt associated with a measurement result.
