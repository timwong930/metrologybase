Quick glossary

The terms people use
without explaining.

A dedicated glossary route is now part of the shared site structure, ready for searchable term pages later.

Accuracy

How close a measurement is to the accepted or true value.

Precision

How closely repeated measurements agree with one another.

Traceability

An unbroken, documented chain of calibrations back to recognized references.

Uncertainty

A quantified statement of doubt associated with a measurement result.

Tolerance

The allowed deviation from a specified nominal or target value.

TUR

The ratio between the DUT tolerance and the uncertainty of the calibration process.

Why MetrologyBase

A reference built for people who are still learning while doing the work.

Metrology can become jargon-heavy very quickly. MetrologyBase is meant to bridge the gap between a textbook, a manufacturer manual, and the practical question you need answered right now.

Content is educational and independent. Product specifications and standards should always be verified against their official sources.
