Quick glossary

The terms people use
without explaining.

Plain-language definitions designed to help you understand a conversation, specification, or procedure faster.

Accuracy

How close a measurement is to the accepted or true value.

Precision

How closely repeated measurements agree with one another.

Traceability

An unbroken, documented chain of calibrations back to recognized references.

Uncertainty

A quantified statement of doubt associated with a measurement result.

Tolerance

The allowed deviation from a specified nominal or target value.

TUR

The ratio between the DUT tolerance and the uncertainty of the calibration process.
