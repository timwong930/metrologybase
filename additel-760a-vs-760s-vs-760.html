Additel 760A vs. 760S vs. the original 760: what actually changed?

The 760A directly replaces the original ADT760. The 760S is a new, simpler version focused on generating and controlling pressure. All three are handheld automatic pressure platforms, but they are not interchangeable if you need electrical calibration or HART.

8-minute guide · Manufacturer-sourced specs · Photos credited below

The short answer

Buy the 760A when you need an all-in-one automatic pressure and electrical calibrator for pressure transmitters, switches and field calibration. Choose the 760S when you already have a reference electrical meter or only need automated pressure generation and control. If you own an original 760, the main practical reasons to upgrade are higher MA pressure coverage, the new capacitive interface, the available contamination prevention system and full HART field-communicator functionality on -DL models.

What changed from the original 760?

1. MA pressure generation rises from 300 to 375 psig

This is the clearest hardware capability improvement. The original 760-MA topped out at 300 psig (20 bar.g), whereas the new 760A-MA and 760S-MA reach 375 psig (25 bar.g). The LLP and D platform maximum ranges remain unchanged. This does not automatically make a 760A more accurate at ordinary calibration points: the precision claim remains up to 0.01% FS and depends on the installed ADT155 module.

2. The touchscreen is updated

The 760A/S moves to a modern capacitive touchscreen with a smartphone-like workflow. This is a usability improvement, not a new pressure-accuracy class. If your original 760 reliably handles your routine tests, the screen alone may not justify replacing it.

3. An optional contamination prevention system is introduced

Additel's new ADT9231 contamination prevention system (CPS) helps keep contamination from the connected device or line out of the pump/controller. This matters when moving between multiple pressure transmitters in industrial service. It is optional, not a standard built-in feature or proof that the older 760 cannot be used safely with clean lines.

4. HART functionality becomes a more substantial communicator

The old ADT760 offered optional HART communication, logging and task documentation on -DL versions. The newer 760A-DL adds a full HART field communicator. Do not compare a base 760A against an old 760-DL and assume the newer base model includes HART: the suffix still matters.

760A vs 760S: why the S is not simply a cheaper A

Both new versions use the same LLP, D and MA pressure-platform envelopes, offer comparable pressure accuracy with the same module, and automate pressure generation/control. The 760S omits the electrical terminals and device-communication functions. It does not directly measure or source current and voltage, provide 24 V loop power, or offer the -DL HART/data-logging/task package. That separation changes how many instruments you need on a transmitter-calibration job.

Real-world example: To check a 4–20 mA pressure transmitter, a 760A can generate the test pressure and measure the loop signal in one instrument. A 760S can generate the pressure, but you need a separate electrical meter or calibrator to read the transmitter's current output.

Which model and pressure version should you choose?

Choose ADT760A-DL for full transmitter workflows

Best when your technicians routinely document as-found/as-left results, communicate with HART instruments, need loop power, or want one primary field calibrator. Choose an internal ADT155 module range that fits your normal test points.

Choose standard ADT760A when you need pressure + electrical

Useful when mA measurement/source, loop power and pressure-switch tests are necessary, but a separate documenting/HART system already handles those tasks.

Choose ADT760S for pressure-only workflows

Good for calibrating mechanical gauges, doing automated pressure cycling and generating stable pressure while a separate meter handles process signals. Avoid it when your goal is one-instrument transmitter calibration.

Keep your original 760 if it already meets your requirements

For work below 300 psig without a need for the newer touchscreen, optional CPS or enhanced HART communicator, replacement may not offer a meaningful metrology benefit. The original unit already has automated pressure generation, interchangeable pressure modules and optional documenting features.
