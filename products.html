How we evaluate gear

There is no universal “best.”

01Measurement problem

What quantity, range, medium, tolerance, and environment are you actually dealing with?

02Measurement capability

Accuracy, uncertainty, stability, resolution, and turndown matter more than headline specs alone.

03Workflow

Automation, documentation, HART, data transfer, and accessories can matter as much as raw performance.

04Total ownership

Calibration cost, support, repairability, modules, software, and future expansion all affect value.
