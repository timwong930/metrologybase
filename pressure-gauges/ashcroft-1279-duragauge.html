Watch for

Verify before buying

Selection is highly configuration-dependent: media, pulsation, process connection, fill, wetted material and mounting all matter.

Calibration perspective

Grade 2A is not a reference-gauge specification.

Use this as process instrumentation unless your procedure and uncertainty analysis support otherwise. Mechanical indication, environmental effects and range selection all matter.
