Why the 685 is different

More than a digital gauge.

The features that materially change how it fits into calibration and field workflows.

Gauge + absolute

Built-in barometer

The standard built-in barometer is specified at 55 Pa and lets a pressure model display gauge or calculated absolute pressure from the same instrument. Additel also lists dedicated BP and BPH barometric ranges at 55 Pa and 22 Pa.

Workflow

10 million record logger

The optional DL configuration stores date, time, pressure and temperature. Logging intervals are user-selectable from 0.1 to 9,999.9 seconds, so the 685 can operate as a stand-alone field logger.

Interface

Touchscreen + rugged housing

The 3.4-inch capacitive touchscreen uses a replaceable tempered-glass protector. The enclosure is 304 stainless steel, carries an IP67 rating, and is intended for lab or field use.

Range & accuracy guide

What changes as pressure goes up.

The 685 is not one accuracy specification across every range.

Gauge and compound ranges

  • Gauge ranges span V15 / GP2 through GP60K.
  • Compound ranges run from CP2 through CP1K.
  • 0.01%, 0.02% and 0.05% FS are available on many low-to-mid ranges.
  • 0.1% of reading is available on supported gauge and compound ranges; below 20% of range it reverts to ±0.02% FS.
  • Gauge ranges above 1,000 psi are specified as sealed gauge.

High pressure and differential

  • 15,000 psi and above use 1/4HP Autoclave F-250-C or M-250-C style connections.
  • 20,000–40,000 psi ranges are published at 0.05% FS with 0.1% FS ordering capability shown in the table.
  • 50,000–60,000 psi ranges use 0.1% / 0.2% FS options.
  • Differential ranges cover ±1 to ±300 inH₂O and use gas media with barb connections.
  • DP100, DP150 and DP300 are specified at 0.02% FS; lower DP ranges are 0.05% FS.
Connections & communications

What you actually have to order.

The suffix choices matter as much as the headline pressure range.

Process ports

Standard pressure

Below 15,000 psi: 1/4 NPT male, 1/2 NPT male, 1/4 BSP male, 1/2 BSP male or M20 × 1.5 male. Other connections can be requested from Additel.

High pressure

15,000 psi+

Choose AF for Autoclave F-250-C female or AM for Autoclave M-250-C male. Both use the 9/16-18 UNF high-pressure interface specified by Additel.

Digital / analog

USB-C and Bluetooth are standard

RS-232 is optional on ADT685 and ADT685Ex. RS-485 and 4–20 mA output are optional only on the non-Ex ADT685.

Best fit

Where it makes sense

Calibration labs, field technicians and process users who want a modern touchscreen reference gauge, absolute-pressure capability via the built-in barometer, broad pressure coverage and optional local data logging.

Watch for

Verify before buying

Accuracy, connection type and communication options are range/model dependent. ADT685Ex removes RS-485, 4–20 mA and external-power options, and high-pressure ranges require different process connections.

Calibration perspective

Do not compare 0.01% FS and 0.1% RD as if they were the same thing.

For the RD option, Additel specifies ±0.02% of full span from 0% to 20% of range and ±0.1% of reading from 20% to 110%. For compound-pressure vacuum, the datasheet gives a separate ±0.25% FS statement. Compare the exact ordered range and accuracy basis against your DUT tolerance and calibration uncertainty at the actual test points.
