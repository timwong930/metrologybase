Field-use details

Why the 680A is useful in the field.

It is designed more like a durable working gauge than a bench-only pressure reference.

DISPLAY

Readable and adjustable

Wide-view FSTN LCD with white backlight, switchable 4- or 5-digit display and 14.5 mm (0.57 in) numerals. Default update rate is 3 readings/s, adjustable from 10 readings/s to one reading every 10 seconds.

BATTERY

Very long runtime

Three AA alkaline batteries. Additel lists 4,000 hours at the default 3 readings/s in super-stable mode and up to 18,000 hours at one reading every 10 seconds in low-power mode.

RUGGEDNESS

Built for field work

IP67 protection for liquid media, 5 g vibration resistance, 10 g shock resistance and a 1 m drop test. The sensor is fully welded.

LOGGING

Long unattended runs

The optional DL configuration stores up to 800,000 time + pressure records or 400,000 time + pressure + temperature records, with intervals from 1 to 99,999 seconds.

COMMUNICATIONS

Bluetooth + RS-232

Bluetooth range is listed at 20 m unobstructed, with Additel Link mobile-app support. RS-232 can also provide external power. Do not use RS-232 in a hazardous area.

UNITS

Broad unit support

Pa, kPa, MPa, psi, bar, mbar, kgf/cm², %, inH₂O, mmH₂O, inHg, mmHg, six engineering water-column references and one custom unit through Additel Link.

Accuracy perspective

0.1% FS is range-dependent.

Because the specification is full-scale based, selecting an unnecessarily large range directly increases the allowed pressure error.

Example · 300 psi range

GP300 at 0.1% FS

The headline accuracy corresponds to ±0.3 psi across the range. At 0.2% FS, that becomes ±0.6 psi.

Example · 10,000 psi range

GP10K at 0.1% FS

The headline accuracy corresponds to ±10 psi across the range. At 0.2% FS, that becomes ±20 psi.

For calibration work, choose the smallest practical range that still covers the DUT and required overpressure margin. Then confirm your TUR or uncertainty at the actual test points.

Connections & construction

Know the port before you order.

The connection changes once the pressure range moves above 15,000 psi.

≤ 15,000 PSI

Standard threaded ports

N = 1/4 NPT male, N2 = 1/2 NPT male, B = 1/4 BSP male, B2 = 1/2 BSP male, M = M20×1.5 male.

> 15,000 PSI

1/4HP connections

AF = Autoclave F-250-C female, 9/16-18 UNF-2B. AM = Autoclave M-250-C male, 9/16-18 UNF-2A.

MATERIALS

Stainless construction

304 stainless-steel shell with 316L stainless wetted parts. Overall size is 105 × 46.5 × 148.5 mm and weight is approximately 0.58 kg / 1.27 lb.

680A vs 681A

Need better accuracy or more pressure-reference capability?

The ADT680A is the simpler 0.1% / 0.2% FS field gauge. Move up to the ADT681A family when you need substantially better accuracy, additional pressure types or a more reference-oriented calibration tool.

Best fit

Choose the ADT680A when

You need a rugged field gauge, mechanical-gauge replacement, long battery life, Bluetooth or long-duration logging, and 0.1% / 0.2% FS is sufficient for the job.

Watch for

Do not oversize the range

Full-scale accuracy gets less useful as the selected range grows. For calibration work, confirm the exact range supports your required TUR or uncertainty at each test point.
