Lowest differential ranges

Use LLP below ±30 inH₂O, or D where gauge/differential tests reach 35 psi.

Industrial transmitters

Pick the lowest suitable 500/1K/1.5K platform for the maximum generated pressure required.

Barometer testing

Select BP for absolute barometer calibration; it is not a general-purpose gauge source.

Shared capabilities

Integrated automatic pneumatic pump and controller, removable ADT155 modules, two external module inputs, electrical source/measure, HART and PROFIBUS support, pressure-switch test, logging and task documentation. Manufacturer lists a 7-inch touchscreen and approximately 8 hours of typical battery life.

General accessories include charger, battery, test leads, calibration certificate and carrying case. The LLP includes a DP holder; the 1.5K uses ADT111-X-KIT manifold and its own hose kit instead of the standard ADT106A assembly.

Other Additel platforms

For a more compact handheld automatic calibrator, review the 760A / 760S. For automated hydraulic calibration at higher pressures, compare the 762. These are separate products with different operating media and ordering rules.
