Choosing the right tool

Better immersion flexibility for difficult probes.

Food and pharmaceutical RTDs, sanitary probes, short or unusually shaped sensing assemblies, and batches of compatible probes. Understand the physical probe geometry and required uncertainty before choosing by maximum temperature alone.

01 · Wide sensor geometry

90 × 90 mm opening, 155 mm maximum medium depth and 2.5 L maximum volume per manufacturer.

02 · Batch throughput

Manufacturer examples include sanitary sensor fixtures and multi-probe calibration arrangements.

03 · Optional three-channel PC

Reference channel plus two DUT channels, 24 V power and HART support.

04 · Bath-specific engineering

Dual-zone thermal system, fluid overflow control, remote operation and automatic internal sensor self-calibration.

Check before ordering

Setup is as important as the range

Unlike a dry well, a liquid bath requires a compatible heat-transfer fluid, cleaning and transport discipline. The two temperature ranges also call for different recommended silicone oil viscosities.
