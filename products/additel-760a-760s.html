Pick the right platform

LLP, D or MA?

The platform matters more than the headline maximum pressure. It determines the pump/control envelope and which internal ADT155 modules you can use.

Lowest pressure

LLP

Best for low differential and gauge-pressure work where resolution close to zero matters.

  • Platform range: ±30 inH₂O (±75 mbar)
  • Modules down to ±0.25 inH₂O
  • Barb fitting connection
Middle range

D

Best for common differential/gauge transmitter work without jumping to the larger MA pressure envelope.

  • Platform range: -12.5 to 35 psi
  • 0.01% / 0.02% FS compound modules
  • Differential modules through the DP1K range
Broadest range

MA

Best when you need vacuum through higher pneumatic pressure and gauge-to-absolute capability.

  • Platform range: -12.5 to 375 psig
  • Built-in 55 Pa barometric reference
  • Gauge / absolute operation

760A vs 760S

What you give up with the S.

Both versions retain automated pressure generation, the touchscreen, communications and swappable pressure modules. The difference is the process-calibrator workflow around the pressure source.

Choose ADT760A

When you calibrate transmitters end-to-end

The 760A adds the electronic channel for mA/V measurement, mA source/simulation, 24 V loop power and pressure-switch testing. Choose -DL when you also need HART, data logging and task documentation.

Choose ADT760S

When pressure generation is the job

The 760S is the simplified non-PC version. It keeps automated pressure generation/control but omits the 760A electrical source/measure channel and does not offer the -DL documentation package.
