Pneumatic & hydraulic pressure pumps

Handheld, portable and benchtop comparison pumps from very low pressure through 60,000 psi.

Official Additel pump line ↗Back to Additel products
Choose the pump by pressure range, pressure medium, portability and the volume you need to pressurize. Pneumatic pumps use air; hydraulic pumps use liquid media and are the practical choice once pressure requirements move into the higher-pressure ranges.
Range at a glance±6 psi to 60,000 psi

The line spans ultra-low-pressure pneumatic comparison work, portable field pumps, high-pressure pneumatic generation and ultra-high-pressure hydraulic bench calibration.

Selection overview

Choose the pressure medium first.

Pneumatic pumps are clean and convenient for lower pressure ranges. Hydraulic pumps trade air for liquid media to reach much higher pressure efficiently.

Pneumatic

Models 901B, 912A, 914A, 916A, 917, 918, 919A, 920/920HV and the newer 992/993 handheld family.

Coverage: very low pressure through 3,000 psi.

Hydraulic

Models 925, 927, 928A, 946A, 959A, 960 and the newer 997 handheld pump.

Coverage: portable field work through 60,000 psi.
