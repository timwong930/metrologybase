Choosing the right tool

Higher-reference performance for demanding temperature work.

Metrology laboratories, comparison calibrations, precision RTD work and field jobs where uncertainty budgets demand tighter thermal-source behavior. Understand the physical probe geometry and required uncertainty before choosing by maximum temperature alone.

01 · Reference-focused performance

The 878-160 specifies ±0.005°C temperature stability over 30 minutes, not an overall system uncertainty.

02 · PC measurement option

Reference PRT input, two DUT channels for RTD/TC/electrical/switch work and HART document/control support.

03 · Special calibration programs

Optional triple-point-of-water realization kit for the 160; automatic PRT annealing on the 700.

04 · Longer immersion

Manufacturer lists 160 mm for low model and up to 193 mm for higher-temperature models.
