What can the ADT227 do?

Electrical calibration

Source and measure DC current, voltage, resistance and frequency. Measure up to 300 V AC on supported non-Ex inputs; true-RMS AC measurement on standard versions.

Temperature calibration

Thermocouple and RTD simulation/measurement, resistance readings and improved thermocouple cold-junction compensation.

Pressure workflows

Two external pressure-module connections, built-in barometer, pressure leak testing, safety-valve testing and high-static differential-pressure application.

Transmitter checks

Power and measure 4–20 mA loops, compare a transmitter signal against an external reference, and create as-found/as-left records.

Documentation

Save up to 10,000 documenting tasks in standard models; supported apps calculate pass/fail and hysteresis and export to Additel ACal.

Connectivity

USB-C, Bluetooth (up to 20 m unobstructed as specified), timestamped recording and data logging for non-Ex models.

Choose the right 227 version

ADT227

Standard multifunction documenting calibrator with non-Ex electrical functions, touchscreen, pressure-module ports and task storage.

ADT227-HART

Adds a full HART communicator and device-description library. Check transmitter DD compatibility before ordering.

ADT227Ex / Ex-HART

Intrinsically safe variants for appropriately certified hazardous locations. Different electrical limits, 22 V / 25 mA loop supply and 1,000-task storage; Ex-HART functionality differs from the non-Ex full communicator.

Important: The ADT227 does not generate pneumatic or hydraulic pressure. To calibrate a pressure transmitter you still need a suitable pressure source, an ADT161/ADT161Ex reference module and appropriate fittings. Use only compatible intrinsically safe accessories in hazardous areas.

Built for field work

Environmental and field details

IP67 housing, 1 m drop-test specification, 5-inch 480 × 800 touchscreen, removable lithium battery, USB-C communications, and a stated operating temperature range of −10 to 50 °C for standard ADT227 (−20 to 50 °C Ex).

Standard battery: 6,600 mAh, 23.8 Wh. Ex battery: 4,000 mAh, 14.4 Wh. The current PDF's stated operating time is up to 12 hours; avoid assuming runtime under every load condition.

Practical applications

4–20 mA transmitters

Supply loop power, measure actual mA output and calculate transmitter error against a calibrated stimulus.

RTDs and thermocouples

Simulate or read sensors and verify controllers, indicators and temperature transmitters.

Pressure transmitters

Pair an external ADT161 module with a compatible pneumatic or hydraulic pump. Build pressure-to-current calibration reports.

See our Anderson-Negele SL calibration with the ADT227 for a full example.

Included and optional accessories

The datasheet lists test leads, pressure-module cable, charging accessories, carrying/hanging strap, battery and ISO 17025-accredited calibration certificate among the included items (model dependent). Optional accessories include ADT161 pressure reference modules, the ADT129 manifold, and the 9082 250 Ω HART adapter. Check the ordering table for differences between standard and Ex.
