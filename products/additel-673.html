ADT673

Advanced digital pressure calibrator. One portable reference for pressure measurement, DC mA or voltage measurement, transmitter loop power and — with the optional HART configuration — digital transmitter communications.

Best suited to field technicians who need to see applied pressure and transmitter output together. The ADT673 measures pressure using its internal sensor; it does not contain a pump or automatically generate pressure.

Pressure measurement: accuracy depends on the exact range

Do not interpret “0.02% FS or 0.1% reading” as a universal choice for every ADT673. The available pressure modules, media, connection type and accuracy codes vary by pressure range. FS here refers to the configured pressure span; RD refers to the measured reading.

Measurement, not sourcing: the ADT673's electrical section measures mA and V and supplies transmitter loop power. Its datasheet does not specify independent, precision mA or V signal-generation functions. Use an electrical source calibrator when the test requires injecting a known signal into a receiver.

For a conventional transmitter, compare the reference pressure shown on the ADT673 with its measured current or voltage. The electrical channel supports scaling, logging, and task documentation; voltage/current selection is described in manual §2.4.

HART: what is optional, and what it adds

Without the HART option

Measure the actual analog loop current (or DC voltage) and compare it against the applied pressure. You can verify analog output even if the field transmitter itself happens to support HART, but you cannot assume protocol-based device configuration is available.

With the HART option

Communicate with supported HART pressure transmitters; read process variable (PV), analog output (AO), percent output and device parameters. Supported procedures include loop test, range settings and device-dependent sensor or D/A trim. The full communicator uses device description files; available commands vary by device.

HART operations are detailed in manual §2.5 and chapter 8. Confirm the purchased configuration includes the HART communicator and that the target device is supported.

Control-system isolation: Additel instructs users to separate the HART device from the control system before using the ADT673 as HART master. For live plant loops, use approved maintenance and isolation procedures; never assume a communicator is safe to attach without an operational review.

Field hardware, connections and documentation

Hardware and connectivity

  • 3.4-inch capacitive color touchscreen and replaceable screen protector.
  • USB-C and Bluetooth included; Wi-Fi or RS-232 optional.
  • 8 GB data storage; manual and automatic logging.
  • Rechargeable Li-ion battery, stated typical 16-hour battery life; actual run time is affected by loop-power use.
  • Operating temperature −10 to 50 °C; IP67 enclosure.

Pressure ports and media

  • At less than 15,000 psi, manufacturer lists NPT, BSP and M20 × 1.5 options.
  • At 15,000 psi and above, designated 1/4HP male or female high-pressure connections are listed.
  • Low differential models use barb fittings; medium is range-dependent.
  • Overpressure warning at 120% is an alarm setting, not permission to exceed the hardware's working rating.

For 60,000 psi tests, verify that every fitting, hose, adapter and pressure source is rated for the applied pressure. See the high-pressure fitting guide.

Ordering and included accessories

The manufacturer ordering structure includes accuracy class, pressure-range code, selected units, pressure port, optional communications and optional HART communicator. An illustrated example uses ADT673-02-GP300-PSI-N with optional Wi-Fi/HART selections. Model suffixes and permitted combinations must be confirmed from the current ordering guide.

Listed in the box: rubber boot, test leads, rechargeable battery, USB-C cable, power adapter and ISO/IEC 17025-accredited calibration certificate. Differential models include silicone tubes. Optional items: 9903 carrying case, 9040 magnetic strap, 9257 rain cover, 9260 screen-protector kit, ACal software and compatible logging software. Confirm exact accessories at purchase.
