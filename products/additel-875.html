Choosing the right tool

Portable, fast temperature points without a liquid bath.

Field calibration of common industrial temperature probes, indicators and transmitters with compatible insert sizes. Understand the physical probe geometry and required uncertainty before choosing by maximum temperature alone.

01 · Dual-zone control

Designed to improve axial and radial temperature uniformity.

02 · Optional PC electronics

External reference PRT plus two DUT inputs for RTD/TC, electrical and switch tests; HART and documentation.

03 · CYOR range option

Choose Your Own Range can optimize and calibrate a custom range within the selected model's operating envelope.

04 · Dry-block accessories

Model-appropriate metal inserts, optional PRTs and a short-probe kit for the low-temperature model.

Model ranges

Choose the temperature envelope.

Each variant is a different temperature source. Display accuracy and stability are model-specific and should not be read as overall calibration uncertainty.
