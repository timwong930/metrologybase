# Buying guides

Good buying decisions start with use case, not brand.

## Questions to ask

- What measurement task am I solving?
- What range do I actually need?
- What tolerance or uncertainty matters?
- What environment will the equipment live in?
- What calibration or maintenance burden comes with it?

## What to compare

- Measurement range
- Resolution
- Accuracy claim
- Stability
- Warm-up or settling time
- Ease of calibration
- Support for the units and fittings you need

## Simple rule

Buy for the measurement problem you have now, and leave room for the next one if the cost is reasonable.
